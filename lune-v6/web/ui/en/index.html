Heating mode, optional min. total opening, and heat-pump openings. In heat-pump mode every loop keeps flow so the heat pump can run at the lowest flow temperature: a room that needs heat opens to the demand opening (80 %), a room at setpoint holds the base opening (60 %), a warmer room trims to the trim floor (35 %) and only closes when it is clearly overheated and the manifold supply is warmer than the room. Openings are scaled per loop by the hydraulic balance (area, pipe spacing, pipe type).
